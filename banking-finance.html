UPI Safety Guide

Simple habits to help protect your payments.

  • Never share your UPI PIN or OTP—even with someone claiming to be your bank.
  • A PIN is used to send money, not to receive it. Check the request before approving.
  • Verify the recipient name and amount. Don't scan unknown QR codes to receive money.
  • If a payment seems suspicious, contact your bank using its official app or number.
